Le

Geographer and GIS practitioner, Melbourne

I work where spatial science meets community knowledge. My research looks at how people adapt to bushfire and environmental change, and how mapping can carry the values and voices of the communities it describes.

I hold a Master of Geospatial Science from RMIT University and a Bachelor of Environmental Geography with First Class Honours from the University of Melbourne. I have worked on participatory mapping with Gajerrong Traditional Owners in northern Australia and remote sensing for an ACIAR-funded project in Cambodia. I am a Board Director at Geography Victoria.
